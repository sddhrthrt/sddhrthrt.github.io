Hi there! Thank you for your curiosity about me, Sid. My full first name is Siddhartha. Yes, I have read _that_ book.

I'm a senior software developer. It's an odd time to be one: I still like reading my codebase and bouncing around in the IDE to build a castle in my head of how the code works. I also, sometimes, spin up a `pi`, select a model from `opencode-go`, and ask it to 

> _brief me about the downsides of using `<Form>` elements rather than individual fields in the New Appointment page_ 

or something like that. However, if Gabriel is on discord, I'd rather ping him first since he wrote that form.

I like thinking about problems, how systems interconnect and depend on each other, prodding and asking questions about how decisions are being made by other people contributing to these systems so I can not just make right decisions, but be in synergy with the community that builds the systems. I believe in teams that share their vision, ways of thinking, inspirations, and safety with each other, and want to build products with them. That's why you'll not find me accepting a job offer to be the star developer that'll deploy an "agent horde" that'll "migrate the microservice singlehandedly from python 2 to rust". I'll cut the boring parts and accelerate debugging with agents, but I still think it's great communities that make great software.

I also believe in building strong connections with people I work with. I coach colleagues when there's something I can offer to them, help mediate friction between people, mentor newly minted software devs or folks new to the team, and foster friendships with those I get close to. I value high trust with my managers, and try to ensure good visibility into how I'm thinking, where I'm stuck and what I'm likely to deliver.

I'm also a photographer. It's my creative outlet, it's my political voice. My work (a little backlogged, but still) is at [Chitrakathe](chitrakathe.com). I'm a balcony gardener, 3D printing nerd, I run my own tech stacks on my NAS when I can replace a paid subscription, I bake pizzas when I'm camping, I'll drive up 400km if there's a forecast for northern lights on a clear day. 

I'm open to jobs that are hiring full-time remote positions, or flexible office presence in greater Vancouver area. Please [reach out to me](mailto:sddhrthrt@gmail.com).
