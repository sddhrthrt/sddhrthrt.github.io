At StackAdapt, I was dropped into the backend team, but was managed by the Director of Data. My job was to find how we could correctly and efficiently build up-to-date user profiles from the millions of ad requests the supply-side ad platform handled. 

I learned about debugging issues in a very tight, low-visibility stack. I learned about asking for help when navigating a steep learning curve. I learned about gathering large amount of context for why we are doing something, but keeping my head down to focus on the exact observability metrics that would help me solve the problem. 

Working on a new tech stack (golang, observability stack I can't exactly remember) was great, and I was becoming mature as a developer by now. I was not perfect at asking for help, however, or not panicking when I felt like I was falling behind.
