title: 'Masters at University of Toronto'
pubDate: '2017-09-01'
---

Education at University of Toronto opened the doors to so many new ideas. From saying yes to collaborating with folks way beyond my technical ability, to experiencing Machine Learning 101 with a new level of rigor, I had a great time.

At the end of it, I had learned one thing: I was not made for the academia. I wanted a problem a user was facing, and I wanted to write a feature for it. I had also learned that it was paramount that I get good at communicating with colleagues, stakeholders, and people of all ilk.

I did, however, get to work on [a startup](/003-stackadapt) that went under as soon as I graduated. I also found a stepping stone to Toronto's startup scene.
