title: 'Initial days of in-house model training at Adobe'
pubDate: '2014-10-01'
---

After graduating from undergrad, I was hired to work at Adobe on their multi-channel advertisement spend attribution model. I went from stumbling over github PRs and confusing tab/space conventions to writing a framework that could manage PySpark-based model training loop on Airflow. 

Working in corporate environment, in teams up to 14 or so, collaborating between managers across the org, reusing components and services from sister teams, to convincing folks across the water cooler to adopt our training management framework, I did the full rigamaroll there. When I applied to Masters of Applied Computing program at [University of Toronto](/002-toronto), I didn't know how much I would miss being able to kill at the ping-pong or pool table at lunchtime in Bangalore.
