or something like that. However, if Gabriel is on discord, I'd rather ping him first since he wrote that form.

I'm a balcony gardener, 3D printing nerd, I run my own tech stacks on my NAS when I can replace a paid subscription, I bake pizzas when I'm camping, I'll drive up 400km if there's a forecast for northern lights on a clear day. In other words, I'm your regular ol' programmer dude. 

Nothing special.
